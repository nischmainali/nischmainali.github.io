Paper and light

The surface is a Lokta-like pulp drawn in the browser: one opaque WebGL2 canvas, owned by a worker where the browser allows it, carrying a projected shutter field, fine moving grain, and a sparse raking relief that only resolves under low sun.

The light has two positions rather than two themes. Shade is a covered sheet near a clearer window side; sunset multiplies a warm apricot field across the same geometry and opens the slats. The ink stays daylight in both. The optics are a reconstruction of sunlit.place, re-derived on this paper rather than copied: the shutter is a projective fan with the source's periods, depths, and progressive diffusion, and its one botanical signature is a single niuro frond that enters only at sunset.

A small copy of the live surface is kept for the length of a visit, so a link, a refresh, or a step back through history lands in the same patch of light on the same clock instead of replaying an entrance.

Type

Linden Hill
Prose and titles. A revival of Frederic Goudy's Deepdene, which gives the page its old-style, slightly hand-shaped texture.
Jost
Navigation, metadata, marginal numbers, and register labels — every place the site is speaking about itself rather than to you.
Asana Math
Mathematics, through its OpenType MATH table. Its Palatino lineage keeps open counters and a calligraphic italic beside Linden Hill, where Times-derived math reads institutional and Computer Modern reads like default TeX.
EB Garamond Initials
The optional opening capital, in two layers: a readable letter and an ornamental ground. The ornament is cut into one file per capital, so a note downloads only the initial it prints.

Every face is served from this site under its own license, with the upstream revision recorded beside it. Nothing is fetched from a font network at read time.

Ink

The registration mark in the upper-right crop corner changes the editorial ink and a restrained cast in the paper, while typography, geometry, measure, and the light's choreography stay fixed. There are {{ index $words (len $inks) }} impressions, drawn from the Ef and Modus families and mapped by role rather than by copied hex value, because this text sits on a moving translucent field rather than a flat editor background.

On a first capable visit the browser is assigned one of {{ index $words (len $pool) }} inks and remembers it on the device. Treat that as printing variation: it uses no cookie, account, request, or server record, and choosing an ink replaces the assignment.

Mathematics

Mathematics is typeset during the build and shipped as finished markup. There is no renderer in the page and no request to a mathematics network. Invalid or unsupported TeX stops the build at its source position rather than reaching a reader as raw source.

Numbering is built once from each note's source and scoped to its sections, so a result carries the same label wherever it is named. Every numbered result is collected in the index of statements; recurring symbols live in the notation register. A reference may cross from one note into another, and prints the label and title of what it points at.

Citations come from a small hand-verified registry, and a page's bibliography is assembled at build time from the works it actually cites.

Making

Hugo {{ hugo.Version }} builds the site as static HTML. The visual foundation is a locally modified copy of hugo-paged, whose crop marks were the beginning of everything here; almost none of its original appearance survives. Nothing on the page depends on a framework, a client router, or a third-party script.

The search page reads an index built with the site. Notes print as offprints: the light, the controls, and the marginal positioning drop away, and the hierarchy, mathematics, figures, and citation record remain.
