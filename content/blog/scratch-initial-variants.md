Four treatments of the same opening initial, in the real reading measure and the
real light. The letter is the F2 face, the floral ground is F1, exactly as now;
only their relative size, position, and the ground's strength differ.

The markup below is written by hand rather than through the `dropcap` shortcode,
because the source checker allows one initial per page. **Delete this page once a
treatment is chosen.**
